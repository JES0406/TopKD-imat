What

The question we answer

On MNIST, does adding a topology loss make the Student's latent space take on the Teacher's shape, and does that help accuracy beyond plain knowledge distillation?

Knowledge distillation: a small Student learns from a big pre-trained Teacher by imitating its outputs.

TopKD also makes the Student imitate the topology of the Teacher's latent space: for each batch, the embeddings form a point cloud, its persistence diagram summarises its shape, and a persistence image turns that into a grid the loss can compare.

Why MNIST: it is interpretable. A good Teacher puts the 10 digits in 10 clusters, which shows up as 9 long-lived H0 bars, so we can see whether a diagram is good and whether the Student copied it.

What "good shape" looks like Illustrative H0 barcode of a batch's latent point cloud: short bars are noise inside a cluster; the 9 long bars plus one infinite bar mean 10 well-separated digit clusters.
MNIST batch→ Teacher (~25 layers) · Student (~5)→ latent point cloud per layer pair→ persistence diagram→ exact persistence image→ topology distillation loss

Datasets

  • MNIST — core dataset: full grid, used in the presentation.
  • Fashion-MNIST and similar — extra runs for the memoria only.

Where we differ from the paper

  • Exact persistence images from the course libraries (gudhi, ripser/persim, giotto-tda) — no RipsNet approximation.
  • Topology loss at several layer pairs (Teacher layer 5k ↔ Student layer k), not one.
  • Small, interpretable task instead of CIFAR-100 / ImageNet.

Rules

How we work

  1. main always runs: work on a branch, merge only when make test passes.
  2. Push with make push (runs the tests first). CI re-runs them on every push.
  3. Each subteam owns its folder; cross folders only through the three interfaces.
  4. The consumer writes the contract tests and a stub for what it uses.
  5. No datasets, checkpoints or point clouds in git — DagsHub or HF.
  6. Use the words in CONTEXT.md (it has the Spanish wording for the memoria).
  7. Each owner writes their own memoria section, in Spanish, without generative AI.
